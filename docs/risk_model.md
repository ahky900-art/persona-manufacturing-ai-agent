# Risk model

`R = 100 × (S/5) × (L/5) × (E/5) × (1-C)`

S severity, L likelihood, E exposure are 1–5; C control effectiveness is 0–1. This is a transparent prototype score, not a legally approved risk assessment. Thresholds 15/35/60 are demonstration bands only.

Critical control failures (e.g. maintenance active and LOTO not confirmed) trigger a hard alert independent of the aggregate score. Production use requires site-specific calibration, documented evidence, human review, and false-negative testing.
